This page walks you through everything from your first sign-in to sharing your first form.

## Create an account

Go to your StryveForms URL and open **Sign up**. You need a name, an email address, and a password (at least 8 characters). Already have an account? The **login** page is right there too.

## Your first workspace

A **workspace** is a shared space for a team: its own forms, folders, members, and brand. You get one automatically when you sign up, named after you — rename it any time from **Settings → Workspace**.

If you work with more than one team or client, create more workspaces from the workspace switcher in the top of the sidebar, and switch between them the same way. Each workspace's forms, folders and members are separate.

## Your first form, in 5 minutes

1. From the dashboard, click **New form**.
2. Pick **Start from a template** for a head start (contact form, survey, registration…), or **Blank form** to begin empty.
3. In the editor, click **Add block** to drop in a question — text, email, choice, rating, and 20+ other types. Drag the handle on the left of any block to reorder it.
4. Click a block to open its settings on the right: label, placeholder, whether it's required, and (for choice fields) its options.
5. Your changes save automatically — watch the small status next to the title switch from *Unsaved changes* to *Saved*.

## Publish & share

A new form starts as a **draft** — only you can see it. To make it live:

1. Open the form's **Settings** and set **Visibility** to *Public* (or click the draft warning banner in the Share panel, which does the same thing in one click).
2. Click **Share** in the editor's top bar (or the **Share** tab on the form's page) to get its public link, a QR code, and embed snippets.
3. Send the link, or paste the embed snippet into your own site. See [Sharing & embedding](/docs/sharing) for every option.
