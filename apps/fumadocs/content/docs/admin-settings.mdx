If your account is an **instance admin**, an **Admin** entry appears at the bottom of the sidebar, below your workspaces — these settings apply to the whole installation, not just one workspace. The first account to ever sign up on a fresh instance becomes its admin automatically; see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) for the full setup.

## Access

Controls who can create an account:

- **Open** — anyone can sign up, optionally restricted to a list of **allowed email domains**.
- **Invite-only** — only people with a pending workspace invite can sign up.

A **support email** shown to users who need help is set here too.

## Users

Every account on the instance, across every workspace, with who's an administrator. Toggle the **Admin** switch to promote or demote someone, or **Delete** an account entirely. Anyone with two-factor authentication turned on also gets a **Reset 2FA** button — use it if they lose both their authenticator app and their backup codes; see [Account security](/docs/account-security#if-you-lose-access) for the full recovery path.

## Branding

The instance's own identity — product name, tagline, logo (incl. a dark-mode variant), favicon, and primary colour — covered in full on the [Brand & white-label](/docs/brand#instance-admin-branding) page.

## Emails

Design every outgoing system email (invites, password resets, submission notifications…) in one place: accent, background, text and muted-text colours for both light and dark mode, with a live preview you can switch between templates, colour scheme, and desktop/mobile. **Send test emails** to check real rendering in your own inbox before relying on it, or **Reset to defaults** to start over.

## AI agents & MCP

Controls whether the instance exposes its [MCP server](/docs/mcp-server) and [CLI](/docs/cli) access to AI agents, and manages API tokens and OAuth clients at the instance level. See the MCP server and CLI docs for what agents can do with that access, and [Agent skills](/docs/agent-skills) for task-specific guidance you can hand an agent.
