## Templates

Click **New form → Start from a template** (or go to **Templates** in the sidebar) to see the gallery, grouped into categories like General, Feedback, Events, and HR. Pick one and it opens straight in the editor, already filled in with its fields and design — edit anything, it only becomes a real form once you start changing it.

### Custom templates

Built a form you (or your team) will want to reuse? Open it, and from its menu choose **Save as template**. Give it a name, description, category, color, and icon — it's saved to your workspace and shows up in the gallery alongside the built-in ones, for anyone in the workspace to start a new form from.

You can also start a custom template **from blank**, edit or delete one later, and move it into a folder the same way as a form.

## Folders

Folders organize your workspace's forms (and separately, its custom templates) in the sidebar. To create one, open **Forms** in the sidebar and click **New folder** — give it a name, an optional color, and an icon (any emoji, or a Lucide icon tinted with that color). Folders can be nested.

Drag a form onto a folder to move it in, or use **Move to folder** from the form's menu. A folder's own menu lets you rename it, change its color/icon, or delete it (forms inside move back to "No folder" rather than being deleted).

<Callout type="info">
  Folders are per-workspace. Switching workspaces shows that workspace's own folders and forms, not the ones you just had open.
</Callout>
