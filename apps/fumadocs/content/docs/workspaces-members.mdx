## Workspaces

A workspace is a shared space with its own forms, folders, members, and brand. You always belong to at least one. Switch between workspaces from the switcher at the top of the sidebar; create a new one from the same menu.

Rename or delete a workspace, and pick its icon, from **Settings → General**. Deleting a workspace permanently removes its forms and submissions — you can't delete your only workspace.

## Members

Go to **Settings → Members** to see who has access and invite more people. Enter an email and pick a role — if they already have an account they're added right away; otherwise they get an email invite that expires after 7 days.

## Roles & permissions

Every member has one of three roles:

| Role | Out of the box |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access to everything — always, and this can't be changed. |
| **Member** | Everything except managing members or workspace settings/brand. |
| **Read only** | Can only view forms and submissions (and use the AI assistant). |

An admin can fine-tune exactly what **Member** and **Read only** are allowed to do from **Settings → Roles & permissions** — each row is one capability, toggled on or off per role:

| Capability | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| View forms & submissions | See this workspace's forms and the responses submitted to them. |
| Create & edit forms | Build new forms and change existing ones. |
| Delete forms | Permanently remove a form. |
| Publish & close forms | Make a form live, or stop it from accepting new responses. |
| Manage submissions | Edit, delete, or export submitted responses. |
| Manage integrations & notifications | Connect webhooks, email notifications and other integrations. |
| Manage PDF templates | Create and edit PDF documents generated from submissions. |
| Use the AI assistant | Ask the AI assistant to help build or improve forms. |
| Manage folders & templates | Create, rename and organize folders and saved templates. |
| Manage members | Invite, remove, and change the role of workspace members. |
| Manage workspace settings & brand | Change workspace name, brand, email sending and other settings. |
| Import forms | Bring forms and submissions in from OpnForm or JotForm. |

## Settings overview

Settings are split into three groups, in the same sidebar:

- **Workspace** — General, [Brand](/docs/brand), Members, Roles & permissions, [Email sending](/docs/email-notifications#senders--smtp), Import, [AI assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).
- **Personal** — Account (your name, password, and active sessions) and Developers (personal API tokens, for the [CLI](/docs/cli) and [MCP server](/docs/mcp-server)).
- **About** — version, license, and a link to the source code.

If you administer the whole instance (not just one workspace), see [Admin settings](/docs/admin-settings) for registration, users, instance branding, and the AI agents & MCP tab.
