### My form isn't accepting submissions

Check, in order: its **Visibility** isn't Draft or Closed, its **close date** (if any) hasn't passed, its **submission limit** (if any) hasn't been reached, and — if it's password-protected — that the respondent has the password. All of these live in [Form settings](/docs/form-settings).

### I can't sign up / "registration is closed"

The instance admin has set sign-ups to **invite-only**, or restricted them to specific email domains. Ask whoever administers your instance for an invite — see [Workspaces, members & roles](/docs/workspaces-members#members) for how invites work, or [Admin settings](/docs/admin-settings#access) if that's you.

### My notification/integration email never arrives

- Check **Settings → Email sending**: if you've turned on custom SMTP, confirm the host, port, encryption, username and password are correct, and that your mail provider allows sending from the address you've set as a sender.
- A "From" address only works if your mail server is allowed to send as it — otherwise most providers reject or silently rewrite it.
- Use **Send test email to me** on the notification itself (see [Email notifications](/docs/email-notifications)) to confirm delivery before trusting it on real traffic.
- Check spam/junk — a brand-new sending domain without proper SPF/DKIM records is the most common cause.

### A webhook/integration stopped working

Open that integration and check its **Delivery log** (see [Integrations](/docs/integrations#test-send--delivery-log)) for the actual error from the receiving end — a wrong URL, an expired auth token, or a receiver that's down are the usual causes. **Send test** reuses the exact same delivery code as a real submission, so it's the fastest way to confirm a fix worked.

### The AI assistant says no provider is configured

A workspace admin needs to connect an AI provider and key first, from **Settings → AI assistant** — see [the AI assistant](/docs/ai-assistant#connecting-a-provider). Each workspace needs its own key; it isn't shared across workspaces.

### Can respondents edit or delete their own submission?

Only if you turn on **Let respondents edit their submission** in [Form settings](/docs/form-settings#submissions-what-happens-after-someone-submits) — it adds an "Edit" button to the thank-you screen, scoped to that one submission. There's no respondent-facing delete; you'd remove it yourself from the Submissions table.

### What happens to uploaded files and submission data if I delete a form?

Deleting a form removes its submissions, uploaded files, integrations, and PDF templates along with it — this can't be undone. If you just want to stop collecting responses, set its **Visibility** to Closed instead.

### Why does my CSV export look slightly different in Excel/Sheets?

Any answer that looks like a spreadsheet formula (starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@`) is automatically escaped on export, to stop it from executing as a formula when opened — this is a standard spreadsheet-safety measure, not a bug.

### Where do I manage API tokens for the CLI or MCP server?

**Settings → Developers**, under the **Personal** group. See the [CLI](/docs/cli) and [MCP server](/docs/mcp-server) docs for what you can do with one.

### I switched workspaces and now see different forms

That's expected — forms, folders, members, and brand are all per-workspace. Use the switcher at the top of the sidebar to confirm which one is active; see [Workspaces, members & roles](/docs/workspaces-members#workspaces).

### Still stuck?

Check [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) for deployment-specific issues, or open an issue with reproduction steps on the project's GitHub repository (linked from **Settings → About**).
