## Columns

Click **Columns** to show or hide a field, and drag entries in that menu to reorder them — the table remembers your choice per form. Hidden fields and layout blocks never show up as columns.

## Filters and search

- The **search box** matches any answer across every visible field.
- The **status** dropdown filters to completed or partial submissions only.
- **Date range** narrows the table to submissions received in a window — the same range also drives [Analytics](/docs/analytics)'s traffic chart when you look at that tab next.

## Submission detail

Click a row to open it in a side panel: every answer, formatted for its field type (files as download links, matrices as a small grid, and so on).

From there you can:

- **Edit answers** — correct a typo or update a value on the respondent's behalf.
- **Delete** — permanently remove the submission.

## Export

**Export CSV** downloads the submissions currently shown — respecting your search, filters, date range, and selection, and using only the columns currently visible in the table. Values that look like spreadsheet formulas (starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@`) are automatically escaped so opening the file in Excel or Google Sheets can't trigger one.

If the form has a [PDF template](/docs/pdf-templates), you can also bulk-download every matching submission as a ZIP of individually generated PDFs — the **Download as ZIP** button sits alongside Export CSV once a template exists, and offers a template picker if the form has more than one.

## Bulk actions

Select rows with the checkboxes to delete several submissions at once, or to scope an export/ZIP download to just that selection.
