Two tabs on a form's page turn raw submissions into something you can actually read.

## Analytics

Four headline numbers — **Views**, **Submissions**, **Completion rate**, and **Average completion time** — followed by a **Traffic over time** chart you can switch between date ranges, and a breakdown of **Traffic sources** (direct, referral, social, email…) and **Countries**.

A "view" is counted whenever someone loads the public form page; "completion rate" is submissions divided by views for the selected range.

## Summary

The **Summary** tab gives every question its own card, shaped to fit the question type:

- Choice questions (dropdown, single/multiple choice) show a **distribution**: each option with its count and percentage.
- Number, rating, scale and slider questions show **min / average / max**.
- Date questions show a date-range summary.
- Checkboxes show a yes/no split.
- Matrix questions show their own grid breakdown.
- Open text questions (text, rich text) list recent answers.
