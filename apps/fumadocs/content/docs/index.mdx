StryveForms lets you build forms, share them with a link or embed them on your site, and collect answers — without limits or paywalls. It is free software under the **GNU AGPL v3**.

It is a TypeScript rebuild of [OpnForm](https://github.com/OpnForm/OpnForm) and uses the same form model, so you can [move your OpnForm forms and answers over](/docs/migrating-from-opnform) in a few clicks.

## Features

- 25+ question types: text, email, phone (with country picker), rich text, dates and ranges, dropdowns, single and multiple choice, checkboxes and toggles, ratings, scales, sliders, matrices, file uploads, signatures, barcode and QR code scanning.
- Layout blocks: text, images, video, audio, dividers, page breaks and custom HTML.
- Conditional logic: show, hide, require or disable questions based on earlier answers, plus custom validation rules.
- Calculated values (formulas with 38 functions) and `@` mentions of answers in messages, emails and redirect links.
- Two presentation styles: classic multi-page forms and focused one-question-at-a-time forms.
- Five themes, custom colours, fonts, cover images, dark mode, right-to-left layouts and custom CSS.
- Password protection, closing dates, submission limits, captcha (hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA, Turnstile), editable and partial submissions.
- Notifications and automations: email (with your own SMTP), Slack, Discord, Telegram, signed webhooks, n8n, Zapier, Make and more.
- Submissions table with search, filters and CSV export, analytics and per-question summaries.
- Workspaces with admin, editor and read-only members.
- An [MCP server](/docs/mcp-server) and [CLI](/docs/cli) for AI agents to read and edit forms, with [agent skills](/docs/agent-skills) for task-specific guidance.
